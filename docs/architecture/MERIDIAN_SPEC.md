# MERIDIAN Platform Specification & Execution Plan

## Mission
MERIDIAN is an institutional-grade Company Intelligence, Research, Investigation & Decision Platform designed for investment analysts, corporate strategy teams, M&A advisors, and venture investors.

## Key Capabilities
1. **Multi-Specialist Research Engine**: 9 specialized roles (Chief Research Coordinator, Financial Analyst, Forensic Accountant, Market & Industry Specialist, Competitive Intelligence Analyst, Bull-Case Advocate, Bear-Case Advocate, Evidence Verification Specialist, Final Adjudicator).
2. **Structured Adversarial Deliberation**: Rigorous debate mechanics where assertions are challenged, rebutted, and adjudicated rather than smoothed into ungrounded consensus.
3. **Traceable Evidence Provenance**: Every claim is explicitly linked to primary filings, regulatory documents, or authenticated industry intelligence.
4. **Institutional Reporting**: Paper-grade dossiers with executive summaries, risk matrices, scenario models, and citation tracking.
5. **Multi-Tenant Cloud Architecture**: RBAC, organization scoping, persistent PostgreSQL storage, and live SSE event streams.
